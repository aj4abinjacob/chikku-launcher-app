Chikku Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026
Developer: Abin Jacob
Privacy contact: hello@abinjacob.in
Applies to Chikku Launcher, package io.github.aj4abinjacob.chikku, including the first Play testing candidate.

Your home screen and local data

Chikku is an independent Android home-screen replacement. Its core works offline. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK, Chikku account, compulsory sign-in or subscription. Chikku does not operate a server that receives launcher data. Optional online features and applications you open have the separate handling described below.

Chikku reads launchable applications, component/profile identities, labels, icons and permitted app shortcuts to show and open apps. Workspace placements, folders, user-chosen labels, appearance, gestures, drawer groups, hidden-app choices and optional card configuration are stored in private app storage on your device. Chikku does not upload your app inventory or layout. Inaccessible and locked private-profile contents are excluded from launcher surfaces; this release does not implement a Private Space interface. Hiding an app is cosmetic. App lock guards launches made through Chikku; it cannot secure other apps, notifications, widgets or Android Recents.

Optional contacts

When you enable contact results and grant Contacts permission, Chikku reads personal-profile contact names, lookup/action data and available communication-app entries to search on your device and offer actions. Search results are temporary; Chikku does not upload or retain a copy of your address book. Tapping Call opens a dialer; tapping Message or another communication button passes the selected destination to that app. Chikku does not place calls or send messages without your action. Disable Contacts in Search settings or revoke its permission in Android settings.

Optional notification badges and media cards

When you enable badges or save an enabled media card, you may choose to grant Android notification access. Chikku uses notification identity/counts and, where needed, avatars for badges, and active media-session title/artist/playback information and commands for media cards. This state is held in memory for the feature, filtered for accessible personal-profile owners, and is not uploaded or archived by Chikku. Notification text is not stored. Badge Off clears badge state; an enabled media feature may still need the shared listener. Revoke notification access in Android settings to stop both features.

Optional accessibility gestures

Chikku is a launcher, not an accessibility tool. Its optional AccessibilityService performs only user-requested Lock screen, Recent apps, notification shade or Quick settings actions. It requests no accessibility events and cannot retrieve screen content. It does not record, store or transmit accessibility data. Chikku explains this before opening Android accessibility settings; you may refuse and keep using the launcher, or disable the service in Android settings at any time. It does not provide stock system gesture-navigation animations.

Local card conditions and device status

Cards can use day/time, charging status, whether the default network uses Wi-Fi, and an optional Bluetooth audio-connected condition. Bluetooth access is requested only when enabling that condition on Android 12 or later. Chikku does not scan for Bluetooth devices or collect their names, addresses, SSIDs or GPS location. Conditions are observed while the card panel needs them; they do not execute unattended actions. Conditions and device status are not uploaded.

Optional online search suggestions

Online suggestions are off by default. If enabled, typed search text is sent over HTTPS to the selected Google or DuckDuckGo suggestion endpoint while search is active. The provider also receives your network address and ordinary connection metadata. Chikku sends no local app/contact results, app inventory, advertising ID, account identifier or cookies with these requests, and keeps no disk cache of suggestions. Clearing input, closing search or disabling online suggestions cancels requests; this cannot recall data already received by a provider. Disable Online search suggestions in Search or Dock settings to stop future requests.

Google and DuckDuckGo control their processing and retention. Chikku cannot promise ephemeral processing or erase their records. Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . DuckDuckGo policy: https://duckduckgo.com/privacy . DuckDuckGo states that it does not save identifiable search history; that is not a Chikku guarantee about every endpoint. Explicit web searches open your chosen browser/search engine and follow that application's policy and account settings.

Optional city weather

Each weather card has a separate, default-off online choice. City search uses an offline GeoNames list bundled with Chikku; city-search text is not sent to a weather or geocoding service. The list includes cities and administrative seats, not every small place. Previously saved and restored locations are kept even when absent from this list.

When enabled and visible, a forecast request sends the chosen city's latitude and longitude, reduced to at most four decimal places, to MET Norway over HTTPS. The provider also receives your IP address and ordinary connection metadata. A fixed User-Agent identifies Chikku's app/version and public developer website/email, not you. These are manually selected forecast coordinates, not GPS readings of your device. No advertising ID, account identifier, app inventory, city-search text or cookies is added. There is no automatic background weather polling.

Selected city/configuration remains in private app storage and portable backups. Up to 24 re-fetchable forecast responses and their expiry/Last-Modified metadata are cached locally, along with a request-throttle record, so reopening or restarting does not bypass provider freshness or rate limits. This cache is excluded from backups and may be evicted by Android; it is not a weather-history feature. Turn online weather off or remove the card to stop new requests; turning it off does not immediately erase the existing cache. Clear Chikku's cache in Android app settings to remove cached provider responses. Removing a selected location changes current configuration; older backups/recovery copies may still contain it.

MET Norway states that direct-client IP addresses and requested coordinates are stored in API access logs in its data centre in Oslo, Norway. Its privacy policy describes IP logging for security, traffic management, troubleshooting and service statistics, with up to 90-day IP retention for meteorological services that do not require login. This does not establish the retention of every associated log field. Chikku cannot promise request-only retention, anonymity or deletion of those provider logs. Provider terms/privacy: https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService and https://www.met.no/en/About-us/privacy . Forecast attribution: MET Norway; offline city data: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ . Both use CC BY4.0 open data, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Chikku rounds request/display values, summarizes conditions and derives a next-24-hour temperature range. Data licences are separate from service identification/traffic rules. No provider endorsement or delivery guarantee is implied.

Google Discover and other applications

The first Play release offers an optional gesture that opens the installed Google app. It does not embed a feed or use the external Discover bridge. Google controls its app, feed, personalization, accounts, networking and any provider content; see Google's policy above. Chikku does not read or store the feed. Experimental debug builds can use the separately documented overlay integration; that is outside this Play release.

Hosted widgets, icon packs, document providers, browsers, dialers, messaging apps and other applications keep their own policies. A deliberate action can send its required destination to the chosen app, for example a contact action, URL or shared backup. Chikku does not automatically upload widget contents, contact results or notification contents. A document provider you select can be cloud-backed.

Optional local search history

Learn from my searches is off by default. When enabled, Chikku stores permitted successful personal-app launches and selected search actions locally to improve suggestions. Entries can include component/profile or contact lookup identities, shortcut/folder identities, sent query text, last-use times, use scores and hour-of-day counts. It excludes hidden, locked and non-personal apps. It keeps at most 200 entries and 30 queries and drops entries unused for 90 days. History is not sent to suggestion providers or included in backups. You can pause learning, clear history, remove entries, or turn off showing suggestions. Pausing learning can keep earlier entries if you choose to keep them.

Backups, files and recovery

Chikku disables its Android automatic backup and provides user-initiated export/import through the system file picker or share sheet. A backup contains layout/configuration, component/profile references, user-created names and selected city/appearance settings. It excludes notification contents, search history, app-lock state, locked/private-profile contents and live widget IDs. Portable restores allocate fresh widget IDs through consent rather than reusing saved device IDs. A selected dock image remains a file reference; the image and its permission grant are not copied into the backup.

Backup files are not encrypted by Chikku. You choose their destination and any receiving app or cloud provider. Chikku does not upload them to a Chikku server. Delete exported/shared copies from each destination when no longer needed. Private local recovery records and bounded Undo snapshots can preserve earlier state so damaged or interrupted edits can be recovered. Changing a current setting alone does not necessarily erase older recovery copies. Clearing Chikku app storage or uninstalling removes its local stores; exported files, copies held by receiving apps and data held by other apps/providers are separate. Clearing storage destroys your layout, so export anything you want to keep first.

Security, retention and deletion

Private app storage and Android permission controls protect local data; optional Chikku network requests use HTTPS and do not include persistent advertising/device identifiers. No protection is absolute. Local layout/settings remain until changed, reset, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled; recovery copies may remain as described above. Revoking access stops future use of that permission. We do not receive your biometric or device PIN; Android reports authentication success/failure. No Chikku account exists to delete. For provider-held data, use the provider's privacy controls/contact route. Contact hello@abinjacob.in for Chikku privacy questions. If you voluntarily send support material, send only what is needed and redact private details; correspondence is kept while resolving your request and longer only where needed for legal obligations or disputes. There is no automatic diagnostic/crash upload.

Changes

This policy will be updated when Chikku's handling changes. The updated date identifies this version. Optional purchases are not implemented in this candidate. Any future purchase integration will require updated disclosures before distribution.

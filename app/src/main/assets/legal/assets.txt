Weather estimates: Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), https://www.met.no/ and https://api.met.no/weatherapi/locationforecast/2.0/, under CC BY4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Chikku rounds requested coordinates to four decimals, rounds displayed temperatures, summarizes conditions and derives a next-24-hour temperature range. No MET/NRK logos or weather icon artwork is copied. Forecast data is fetched only after opt-in and is cached locally according to provider expiry; it is not bundled with the APK.

Offline city data: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/, CC BY4.0. Extract downloaded15October2026 from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip plus countryInfo.txt and admin1CodesASCII.txt. The bundled derived index has236,192 places: normalized names/aliases, country/admin labels and coordinates; sorted by population with stable GeoNames ID tie-break. This is a modified selection, not all geographic features; smaller/new/renamed places may be absent. No warranty of accuracy, timeliness or completeness. Original inputs/readme/licence, hashes and deterministic regeneration recipe are included in corresponding project source under data/weather/ and scripts/build-weather-cities.py. Full CC BY4.0 terms are available offline in About.
